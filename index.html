ADAM

Not just a website: a public identity, community hub and complete digital membership system for a new airsoft association.

My involvement

As Secretary of the Board, I work across the association's communication and digital infrastructure: website administration, marketing, community communication, member experience and the design of the systems that support the association behind the scenes.

The challenge

A new association needed to establish trust, explain what it was, attract members and actually manage them. At the same time, the website needed to be useful to people who were not members at all: players looking for teams, fields, events and information about airsoft in Central Portugal.

Painting
2019—2020

Small paintings based on stills selected from South Korean music videos. Each image began with a moment chosen for the emotion it conveyed.

By removing those moments from movement, sound and narrative and translating them into paint, I explored how that emotion could remain—or change—inside a static image. The paintings were made independently; visual sets were organised only afterwards.
