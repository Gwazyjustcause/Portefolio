The project

ADAM needed more than a promotional website. The platform had to explain a new association clearly, support membership from registration through renewal, and create useful infrastructure for the wider airsoft community.

01 / PUBLIC WEBSITE

A front door for the community.

The public site brings the association, community directories, membership, events and news into one coherent experience.

Open homepage ↗ 02 / MEMBERSHIP

From interest to member.

A guided path explains membership options, benefits and the online joining process before registration.

See Associa-te ↗ 03 / COMMUNITY

Useful beyond the association.

Public directories help players discover teams and legally authorised fields, with submissions reviewed before publication.

Member experience

Membership became a product, not a spreadsheet.

The member area brings together a digital membership card, QR validation, account data, membership status and history. Behind it, the system supports registration review, renewals and the different states a membership can move through.

Painting
2019—2020

Small paintings based on stills selected from South Korean music videos. Each image began with a moment chosen for the emotion it conveyed.

By removing those moments from movement, sound and narrative and translating them into paint, I explored how that emotion could remain—or change—inside a static image. The paintings were made independently; visual sets were organised only afterwards.

About

One practice,

I'm Gabriela Ferreira, a multidisciplinary visual designer and artist in Portugal. My background moves through Fine Arts and Multimedia into professional visual production.

I work across identity, web, photography, image-making, painting and 3D, choosing the medium around the work rather than forcing the work into one discipline.
